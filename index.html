Research

High vs. Low Volume Resistance Training

June 2025

A 60-day case study on strength, body composition and joint pain in an adolescent male

Designed and ran a 60-day controlled experiment comparing high-volume and low-volume training. Low-volume protocols achieved equal or better outcomes with reduced injury risk.

  • Formulated the hypothesis and controlled for nutrition, sleep and exercise selection
  • Collected daily quantitative data across multiple metrics
  • Identified the limits of bioelectrical impedance measurement and applied statistical corrections
  • Wrote a formal paper covering methodology, results, discussion and practical applications
